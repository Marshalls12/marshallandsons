Why it matters

Three reasons this is not optional.

Reduce fire risk

Lint collects inside the vent and sits in the path of hot exhaust. Cleaning the line removes that buildup.

Use less energy

A clear vent helps the dryer move heat and moisture out faster, which can reduce repeat cycles.

Protect the dryer

Less heat and strain means less wear on the heating element, blower motor, and other parts.

Questions

Straight answers.

How often should I clean my dryer vent?

Many homes schedule cleaning once a year. Heavy use, long vent runs, and repeat drying cycles may mean it should be done more often.

How long does it take?

Most standard appointments take about 45 to 60 minutes.

What is included in the $199?

The vent line, exterior hood, transition hose check, cleanup, and before-and-after photos.

Do you clean rooftop vents?

Yes, when the roof can be accessed safely. Any rooftop add-on is explained before work starts.
